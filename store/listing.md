## Long description (max 1500)

Arcane Tricks is a trick-taking card game of prediction and nerve, played around a candle-lit table in a
wizard's tower.

Each round, everyone is dealt one card more than the last. A card is turned up for trump. Study your hand and bid exactly
how many tricks you'll take. Hit your bid and you score big. Miss, even by one, and you lose points. Four elemental
suits (Flame, Tide, Grove and Sun) are joined by Wizards, which win any trick, and Jesters, which never do.

FEATURES
• Play solo against bot wizards that bid and play with real strategy
• Online tables for up to 6 players: share a 4-letter code and friends join from a Quest or a web browser
• Empty seats are filled by bots, and dropped players can rejoin their seat
• See other players' heads and hands move in VR, and react with quick emotes
• House rules: a forehead round (everyone can see your card except you), hidden bids, a dealer who can’t make the bids add up, and a short game
• Customise your wizard's robe style and hat colour
• Pick up and inspect any card on the table
• Seated or standing play, with automatic table height
• Controllers or hand tracking
• Generative music and spatial sound

No accounts, no ads, no text or voice chat.

## Age rating (IARC questionnaire)

Answer honestly. For this game that means: no violence, no fear, no sexual content, no drugs or alcohol, no
gambling with real money (it's a points-only card game, with no betting and no purchasable currency),
no crude language. **Users can interact:** yes, but only through a fixed set of preset emotes. There is no
free text or voice, no sharing of location, and no purchases (unless you add them later). Expect an
Everyone / PEGI 3 rating.
