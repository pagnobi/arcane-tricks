Arcane Tricks privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Arcane Tricks is a card game you can play in a web browser or as an app on Meta Quest. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not show ads or use analytics. This page explains the little data the game handles.

Stored on your device

The game saves your chosen display name, your wizard's look, your settings (sound, VR height, house rules) and a random ID used to give you your seat back if you drop out of an online game. These stay in your browser's local storage on your device and are never sent to us. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online games

When you host or join an online table:

  • Your display name, wizard look, game moves, emotes and (in VR) head and hand positions are sent to the other players at the table, so they can see you play. Nothing is sent once you leave the table.
  • Players connect directly to each other (WebRTC). To set up that connection, your device's IP address is shared with the other players' devices and with the connection services below.
  • A matchmaking service (PeerJS, peerjs.com) passes connection details between players using the 4-letter room code.
  • If your networks block direct connections, the game's traffic is relayed through Cloudflare's TURN service (Cloudflare privacy policy), or PeerJS's relay if Cloudflare's is unavailable. Relays forward the game's messages; we don't store them.

There is no text or voice chat. Emotes are a fixed set of preset phrases.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share personal information, we don't keep game history on a server, and we don't use cookies or tracking. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Hosting

The game's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic request information such as IP addresses (GitHub privacy statement).

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/pagnobi/arcane-tricks/issues.
